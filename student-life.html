Student Life, Clubs & Youth Corps

National Service Scheme (NSS)

Active Higher Secondary NSS unit involved in community service, blood donation camps, environmental drives, and social welfare projects.

National Cadet Corps (NCC)

Disciplined cadet wing providing leadership training, parade practice, adventure camps, and national integration initiatives.

Student Police Cadet (SPC)

Official SPC unit fostering civic responsibility, road safety awareness, anti-drug drives, and democratic values among students.

School Kalolsavam & Arts Club

Platform for music, dance, drama, painting, and literary competitions in Sub-District, District, and State School Youth Festivals.

Sports & Athletics Association

Coaching for track & field, football, cricket, basketball, and volleyball with regular participation in inter-school meets.

Eco Club & Science Club

Promoting campus biodiversity, waste segregation, solar awareness, and annual science exhibition model building.
