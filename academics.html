Higher Secondary Academic Streams

CURRICULUM OVERVIEW

Plus One & Plus Two Education

S.M.V. Government Model Higher Secondary School offers two-year Higher Secondary courses (Plus One & Plus Two) affiliated with the Board of Higher Secondary Education, Government of Kerala. Students can choose from Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams designed for academic growth and higher education entrance readiness.

Seat Allocation

Single Window System (HSCAP) conducted by the Directorate of Higher Secondary Education, Govt of Kerala.

Plus One Admission Process (HSCAP)

Admissions to Higher Secondary Plus One streams at S.M.V. Government Model HSS are conducted strictly through the HSCAP online single window allotment process managed by the Higher Secondary Education Department, Govt of Kerala.
