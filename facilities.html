Infrastructure & School Facilities

Smart Classrooms

High-tech classrooms equipped with interactive smart whiteboards, digital projector setups, and high-speed internet for modern multimedia teaching.

Science Laboratories

Dedicated, fully-equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology practical laboratories meeting Kerala Higher Secondary Board standards.

Computer Laboratory

Air-conditioned computer center with desktop computers, broadband connectivity, Linux/Windows OS, and programming compilers.

Central Library

Well-stocked reading hall containing reference books, NCERT guides, competitive exam books, Malayalam and English periodicals.

School Auditorium

Spacious assembly hall equipped with sound system and stage facilities for school celebrations, seminars, and PTA meetings.

Playground & Athletics Field

Open sports grounds for football, athletics track, cricket practice, basketball court, and physical education sessions.
