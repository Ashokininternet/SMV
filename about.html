S.M.V. Government Model Higher Secondary School is located at Overbridge, MG Road, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala (PIN: 695001). As one of the prominent government educational institutions in Trivandrum, the school has played a pioneering role in fostering public education, academic discipline, and student development across secondary and higher secondary levels.

The school offers comprehensive academic streams under the General Education Department, Government of Kerala. With state-of-the-art smart classrooms, computer centers, science laboratories, and active co-curricular programs (NSS, NCC, Student Police Cadets), SMV School continues to empower thousands of young minds.

Our Vision

To provide holistic, high-quality, and affordable education that empowers every student with knowledge, ethical values, critical thinking skills, and a commitment to society.

Our Mission

To create a vibrant learning environment supported by modern technology, dedicated faculty, comprehensive science laboratories, and diverse extra-curricular programs.
